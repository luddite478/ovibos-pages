Privacy request

Request deletion of your Ovibos data

You can ask Roman Smirnov, the developer of Ovibos, to delete personal data held by Ovibos.

How to make a request

  1. Email luddite478@gmail.com with the subject “Ovibos data deletion request”.
  2. Tell us whether you use iPhone or Android and provide enough non-sensitive information for us to locate your app records, such as an approximate analysis or purchase date. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, or photos.
  3. We may ask for limited additional information to verify the request and avoid deleting another person’s records. We will confirm the request and tell you when it has been handled, subject to applicable law.

You do not need an Ovibos account to make a request. Delete saved cards and their photos directly in the app; they are stored on your device and are not synced to Ovibos.

Data we can delete

After verifying your request, we can delete backend installation and device-verification records, analysis request records and generated results, and content reports associated with you, when no retention exception applies. We can also delete support correspondence that is no longer needed. Completed analysis results are normally kept for up to 24 hours; Android content reports are scheduled to expire after 30 days. Expiry from the live database may be delayed.

Data we may need to keep

We may retain the minimum purchase, credit, refund, and security records needed to prevent duplicate grants or replay, maintain an accurate balance, investigate fraud, resolve a refund or dispute, or meet legal and accounting duties. We keep these records only as long as the relevant purpose requires. We will explain if this affects your request.

Application logs are configured for 30 days. Deleted records may remain temporarily in encrypted database recovery backups until those backups expire; we do not promise a fixed backup deletion time. Information already held by Google, Apple, Amazon Web Services, or OpenAI is subject to that provider’s own retention practices and must be requested from the provider where applicable.

Read the Ovibos privacy policy for more details. Contact luddite478@gmail.com with questions.
