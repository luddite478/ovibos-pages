Privacy policy

Roman Smirnov operates Ovibos, a photo-based language-learning app. This page explains what happens to photos and related information when you use the app or contact support.

Pre-release status. Ovibos is currently in private testing. The development app uses live AI analysis after you allow photo sharing for analysis. Public purchases are not enabled yet. We will review this policy against the finished purchase system and production service before public release.

Photos and saved cards

You choose or take a photo. The app prepares an upright, smaller JPEG for analysis and removes image metadata from that copy. Before the first analysis upload, the app asks you to allow photo sharing with our backend and OpenAI. If you allow it, the prepared photo is sent to our backend, which sends it and analysis instructions to OpenAI to create a language-learning card. Your choice is saved on your iPhone, so future photos you choose to analyze are sent without another prompt. You can reset this permission in Settings; the app will ask again before the next upload. Our backend processes the photo for that request and does not save the uploaded photo as a file or database record.

If you save a card, its photo and generated text are stored on your iPhone for offline viewing. They are not synced by Ovibos. You can delete saved cards and their photos in the app. Uninstalling the app can remove those local files; Ovibos cannot restore saved cards or photos from its backend after reinstalling.

Technical information

To verify the app and prevent duplicate or abusive requests, our backend stores an installation identifier, App Attest verification information, request identifiers, a hash derived from the uploaded photo and language choices, request status, rate-limit information, and, for completed requests, the generated card and model-usage information. The backend does not store the uploaded JPEG itself. Our hosting provider may process network information such as your IP address and technical logs needed to operate and protect the service.

Completed analysis results are cached for approximately 24 hours to handle duplicate requests; deletion from the database may occur later because expiry is asynchronous. Installation records and requests with uncertain outcomes currently have no automatic expiry. We will set and verify a production retention schedule for these records and server logs before public release. You can request deletion of records we can locate by contacting us.

Purchases and credits

Purchases and credits are not available in the private test build. The planned public version will use Apple's purchase system and a server-side credit ledger. If launched, we expect to process signed purchase information, product and transaction identifiers, refund status, and the remaining credit balance to provide purchases and restore eligible credits. We will update this section to match the implemented system before sales. We do not receive your payment-card number from Apple.

Service providers

Amazon Web Services hosts our backend and database. Apple provides App Attest and, when purchases launch, payment and transaction services. OpenAI processes prepared photos for live AI analysis. Our requests to OpenAI use store=false; this does not mean OpenAI retains no data. OpenAI says API data is not used to train its models unless the API customer opts in, and its default abuse-monitoring logs may contain submitted content for up to 30 days, with exceptions. See OpenAI's data controls.

GitHub hosts these public pages and may record visitor IP addresses for security. If you email support, Google processes your message through Gmail. We do not include advertising or analytics scripts on these pages.

Your choices and requests

You can decline photo sharing before the first upload, reset that permission in Settings, choose which photos to analyze, delete saved cards on your device, and contact us to request access to or deletion of backend records associated with you. Because there is no Ovibos account, we may ask for information needed to find your records and prevent another person's data or credits from being changed. Some purchase or security records may need to be retained for accounting, fraud prevention, or legal obligations after the purchase system launches.

For a privacy question or request, email luddite478@gmail.com. We may update this policy as the app changes and will change the date above when we do.
