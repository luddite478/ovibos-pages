Privacy policy

Roman Smirnov is the operator of Ovibos and the controller responsible for the personal information described here. Ovibos turns photos into language-learning cards. This policy covers the iPhone app, its analysis and credit services, these support pages, and messages you send to support.

Contact: luddite478@gmail.com. Ovibos does not require an email/password account. Identifiers can still link activity to an app installation or Apple app account, even though we do not receive your Apple account email address or password.

Photos and saved cards

You choose or take a photo. The app prepares an upright, smaller JPEG for analysis and removes image metadata from that copy. Before the first analysis upload, the app asks you to allow photo sharing with our backend and OpenAI. If you allow it, the prepared photo is sent to our backend, which sends it and analysis instructions to OpenAI to create a language-learning card. Your choice is saved on your iPhone, so future photos you choose to analyze are sent without another prompt. You can reset this permission in Settings; the app will ask again before the next upload. Our backend processes the photo for that request and does not save the uploaded photo as a file or database record.

If you save a card, its photo and generated text are stored on your iPhone for offline viewing. They are not synced by Ovibos. You can delete saved cards and their photos in the app. Uninstalling the app can remove those local files; Ovibos cannot restore saved cards or photos from its backend after reinstalling.

Only photos you choose to analyze are uploaded; we do not upload your entire photo library. Removing image metadata does not remove personal information visible in the image. Avoid uploading sensitive documents or photos of other people without appropriate permission.

Technical information

To verify the app and prevent duplicate or abusive requests, our backend stores an installation identifier, App Attest verification information, request identifiers, a hash derived from the uploaded photo and language choices, request status, rate-limit information, and, for completed requests, the generated card and model-usage information. The backend does not store the uploaded JPEG itself. Our hosting provider may process network information such as your IP address and technical logs needed to operate and protect the service.

Completed analysis results are retained for 24 hours for duplicate-request recovery; database expiry is asynchronous. Installation and App Attest records, request records with uncertain outcomes, and credit grant, debit, and refund ledger records have no automatic expiry in the current service. CloudWatch application logs are configured for 30-day retention. You can request deletion of records we can locate by contacting us. We review purchase and security records before deleting them so deletion does not break a balance, refund, or fraud investigation.

Application logs record technical outcomes such as request references, response status, duration, error categories, and model usage. They are not intended to contain uploaded photos, generated card text, or signed purchase proofs. Database recovery backups can contain earlier copies of records after deletion from the active database. Their expiry follows the configured backup recovery window; deleting a saved card on your iPhone does not itself delete backend records or provider-held data.

Purchases and credits

Ovibos uses Apple StoreKit and a server-side credit ledger. To provide and restore credits, the service verifies Apple's signed app and transaction information, then stores a hashed Apple app-account identifier, transaction and product identifiers, StoreKit environment, credit grants and spending, and refund/revocation state. Hashing this identifier does not make the associated purchase records anonymous. Apple processes payment; Ovibos does not receive your payment-card number.

The 75 starter credits are granted once per eligible Apple app account. When a verified Apple app transaction reports that the paid app purchase was refunded, Ovibos removes any unspent starter credits from that account. Credits already spent and credits from refill purchases are not removed by an app-purchase refund. A refund of a refill removes that purchase's unspent credits. Balances do not become negative, and grant, spending, and refund records are retained to prevent duplicate grants and reconcile refunds. Refill packs are not Family Shared. TestFlight and App Review use a separate Sandbox service and ledger; Sandbox purchase transactions do not charge real money.

How we use information

  • Provide photo analysis, translations, and request recovery.
  • Verify app integrity, prevent abuse and duplicate requests, and maintain service reliability.
  • Deliver and restore credits, reconcile purchases and refunds, and resolve support cases.
  • Respond to privacy requests and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Ovibos does not sell personal information, use it for targeted advertising, or share it with data brokers. The app does not include advertising or third-party behavioral analytics tools. Operational diagnostics are used to run and troubleshoot the service.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address, the contents of your message, and any attachments or diagnostic details you choose to provide. We use them to respond and investigate your request. Please send only information needed for the issue, and never send your Apple password or payment-card details. Support correspondence has no automatic deletion schedule; we retain it to handle the request and any related dispute or legal obligation. You can ask us to delete correspondence that is no longer needed.

Service providers

Amazon Web Services hosts our backend, database, and technical logs. Apple provides App Attest, payment, and transaction services. OpenAI receives prepared photos and instructions to generate cards. We disable response storage in analysis requests. This does not provide zero data retention: OpenAI's default abuse-monitoring logs may include inputs and outputs for up to 30 days, or longer for legal or safety reasons. Flagged image inputs may be retained for safety review. OpenAI states that API data is not used to train its models unless the API customer opts in. See OpenAI's data controls.

GitHub hosts these public pages and may record visitor IP addresses for security. If you email support, Google processes your message through Gmail. We do not include advertising or analytics scripts on these pages.

When we engage providers to process personal information on our behalf, we require confidentiality, security, and data-protection terms consistent with the protections described in this policy. Providers may also have independent obligations, such as payment, fraud prevention, or legally required safety monitoring. Their applicable terms and privacy notices describe those activities.

International processing

Our analysis service and database are deployed in AWS's Frankfurt region in Germany. Other providers may process information in the United States and other countries, so information may be processed outside your country of residence. Where applicable law requires safeguards for international transfers, those transfers must be covered by an appropriate legal mechanism, such as an applicable adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses. Contact us for details of the safeguards that apply to your information.

Legal grounds for processing

Where a law such as the GDPR requires a legal basis, we rely on your consent to upload chosen photos for analysis; performance of the service you request for credit delivery, restoration, and customer support; and legitimate interests in securing the service, preventing fraud and replay, and investigating technical failures. Our security interests must be balanced against your rights. We also process records where necessary to comply with a legal obligation. Withdrawing photo-sharing consent stops future uploads and does not invalidate processing that occurred before withdrawal.

Your choices and requests

You can decline photo sharing before the first upload, reset that permission in Settings, choose which photos to analyze, delete saved cards on your device, and contact us to request access to or deletion of backend records associated with you. Because there is no Ovibos login, we verify requests using information needed to locate the record and protect another person's balance. Purchase, refund, and security ledger entries do not automatically expire; we may retain the minimum records needed to prevent replay, resolve refunds, and meet accounting or legal obligations. Credit restoration does not restore cards or photos deleted from your iPhone.

Depending on applicable law, you may also request correction, restriction of processing, or a portable copy of your information; object to processing based on legitimate interests; and complain to your local data-protection authority. We respond within the period required by applicable law, normally within one month for GDPR requests, subject to permitted extensions. If we cannot fulfill a request, we explain the reason and any records that must be retained. We do not require an Apple password to verify a privacy request.

For a privacy question or request, email luddite478@gmail.com. We can help identify records using limited information about the app installation, analysis, or purchase. Requests concerning data Apple or another provider independently controls may also need to be directed to that provider.

Security and legal disclosures

Release-service requests use HTTPS. App Attest verification and restricted backend access help protect the service and credit records. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We may disclose limited information when legally required or necessary to investigate fraud, protect rights and safety, or resolve a legal claim.

Children and photos of other people

Ovibos is a general language-learning app and is not designed specifically for children. Where law requires a parent or guardian's authorization for a child's data processing, that authorization is required before uploading photos. If you believe a child has provided information without required authorization, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action. An App Store content age rating does not replace applicable privacy-consent requirements.

Changes to this policy

We update this page and its date when our practices change. For material changes, we provide an appropriate notice, and obtain renewed consent where required before using information for a new purpose. Links to provider pages lead to websites operated by those providers.
