Help and contact

Ovibos support

Questions about photo analysis, saved cards, purchases, credits, or your data? Contact Roman Smirnov, the developer of Ovibos.

Email luddite478@gmail.com

When you write

Describe the problem and, if helpful, include your app version and iPhone model. For a credit or purchase issue, mention the approximate purchase date. Please do not email a sensitive photo unless we ask for it.

If an analysis times out or remains unresolved, include the support reference shown by the app. It identifies the affected request so we can check its outcome without asking you to repeat a paid analysis. Keep the reference private and do not post it publicly.

Privacy requests

Use the same email address to request access to or deletion of backend data. Because Ovibos has no email/password account, we may need to ask for information that lets us locate your records and verify the request. You can delete saved cards and their photos directly in the app.

Read the Ovibos privacy policy for details about photo processing and data storage.
